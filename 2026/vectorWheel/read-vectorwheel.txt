
Two parts we ordered from Mcmaster Carr were the metal dowel pin and the O-ring.
See the actual step file which lists each item in it's assembly.
Metal Dowel is:
1/8" X 1.25" McMaster-Carr # 98381A477 or 98381A477_12

O-ring is:
3/8" ID x 1/8" McMaster-Carr# 1173N204_3

you need 6 dowels and 24 o-rings per wheel.
